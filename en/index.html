How I work

The process, from insight to launch

  1. 01

    Research

    Consumer observation and trend analysis to spot needs and opportunities.

  2. 02

    Concept

    Defining the challenge and a value proposition that links the research findings to business goals.

  3. 03

    Development

    Creating recipes and prototypes to explore alternatives and test technical feasibility.

  4. 04

    Tastings and presentation

    Tastings with consumers and teams to refine the proposal and decide how to move forward.

  5. 05

    Implementation

    Production trials and launch planning with marketing, operations and suppliers.
